# tpt-axiom roadmap

The living task list is [todo.md](todo.md); this page is the user-facing view
of the same plan — what to rely on now, and what is coming.

## Now (works today)

- Uncertainty-propagating arithmetic (`Fuzzy<T>`, `Distribution<T>`) with
  exact first-order propagation rules, statistical helpers, and Monte Carlo
  cross-checks.
- Typed probabilistic decisions: validated `Probability`/`Confidence`,
  first-class abstention, evidence combination in log space, Bayesian
  updates, escalation policies.
- `#[zk_provable]`: plain Rust functions lowered to a backend-agnostic IR,
  with real proofs on halo2 (IPA/Vesta) and arkworks (Groth16/BLS12-381),
  declared-type range checks, exact i128 witness checking, and
  static rejection of circuits that could wrap the field.
- Polynomial-identity verification of circuit lowerings and variance
  formulas (`tpt-axiom-verify`).
- Vendor-neutral interop: Augur distribution conversions, the inference
  sample boundary, external-engine interfaces.

## Next (planned, in rough order)

1. **Usability** — typed input structs generated by the macro, the full
   `cargo axiom` CLI (`inspect`, `check`, `keygen`, `prove`, `verify` with
   JSON inputs), a circuit registry so mismatch detection runs
   automatically, and serialization of keys/proofs with versioned envelopes.
2. **Credibility** — README-as-doctest (done), crate publishing to
   crates.io, CI hardening (MSRV matrix, cargo-deny, coverage,
   `cargo publish --dry-run`), and an external security audit.
3. **Math breadth** — correlated uncertainty (covariance tracking),
   nonlinear transforms on `Fuzzy` (exp/ln/sqrt with second-order
   correction), more distribution families (Uniform, Beta, Gamma, Poisson…)
   with `pdf`/`cdf`/`quantile`.
4. **Verifiable intelligence** — prove a fused estimate or a decision meets
   a threshold without revealing raw inputs; proof-carrying decision
   records.
5. **Reach** — SP1 zkVM adapter once its toolchain is distributable, WASM
   in-browser circuit viewer, on-chain Groth16 verifier.

## Not planned (yet)

Anything that would couple the core to a specific AI vendor, model, or
network service — the foundation stays pure math and metadata by design.
