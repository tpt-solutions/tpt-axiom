Minimum-variance fusion

Two estimates of the same quantity: fusion is provably tighter than plain addition of independent errors. 




Delta method vs. Monte Carlo — exp(x)



  First-order propagation reports exp(mean) and misses the
  curvature entirely; Monte Carlo approaches the exact lognormal answer
  exp(mean + variance/2). Fixed seed, so the result is
  reproducible.




  

  

  

  







Verify a Groth16 proof in your browser



  Real proof verification, not a simulation: a BLS12-381 Groth16 pairing check
  over a real verifying key, run locally in this page. The shipped demo is an
  honest proof of result == a * k with k secret —
  the verifier learns only a = 2 and result = 8, never
  k. Change the claimed result to 9 (or corrupt the proof) and the
  verdict flips to invalid: that is soundness doing its job.
